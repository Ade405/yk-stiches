# Legal and Customer Policies

The documents in this folder are **draft templates** and are surfaced from the storefront footer. They are not legal advice and should not be treated as finalized contracts or a guarantee that every described process is implemented.

## Documents

- [Terms of Use](TERMS-OF-USE.md) — accounts, product descriptions, bespoke orders, payments, and general use.
- [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY-POLICY.md) — categories of data, purposes, integrations, retention, security, and data rights.
- [Cookies Notice](COOKIES.md) — essential sessions and review requirements for any optional tracking.
- [Shipping, Returns, and Alterations](SHIPPING-RETURNS.md) — operational policy checklist for delivery, cancellations, refunds, and fit adjustments.
- [Acceptable Use](ACCEPTABLE-USE.md) — prohibited misuse, uploads, and security reporting.

## Before publishing or accepting live orders

1. Replace the operator/legal entity and address placeholders with verified details.
2. Verify the contact email and phone number; ensure support and privacy requests are monitored.
3. Complete the shipping regions, carriers, delivery fees, production estimates, cancellation cut-offs, refund methods/timelines, alteration scope, and return address. Do not invent a policy just to fill a blank.
4. Verify deployed data flows and enabled providers. In particular, disclose when chat messages/context are sent to Google Gemini, whether Supabase is enabled, how uploaded images and logs are handled, and whether analytics or marketing tools exist.
5. Define retention and deletion periods, data-subject request handling, incident response, access control, and backups. The current application has in-memory data limitations and is not ready to serve as the sole production system for customer records.
6. Confirm that live payment processing exists before describing any payment as collected or verified. The current payment flow is a demonstration/mock flow.
7. Obtain qualified legal review for Nigeria and each other market where customers are served. Review applicable data-protection, consumer-protection, e-commerce, tax, and cross-border transfer requirements.
8. Reconcile the wording of the policies with the real product UI, operational capability, vendor contracts, and customer support process. Re-review whenever the product or data flows change.
