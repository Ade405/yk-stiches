# YK Stitches — Acceptable Use Policy

**Status:** Draft template for review  
**Last updated:** 22 October 2026

Users must not:

- use the service for fraud, impersonation, harassment, unlawful activity, or deceptive orders;
- access another user's account or data without authorization;
- probe, scan, scrape, overload, or disrupt systems without written permission;
- upload malware, unlawful material, or content that infringes another person's rights;
- share another person's personal information or images without an appropriate legal basis or permission; or
- attempt to bypass authentication, rate limits, or other security controls.

Use only images, measurements, messages, and design references you are entitled to share. Report suspected security issues privately to the operator rather than exploiting or publicly exposing them.

The operator should publish a verified security contact, a process for handling reports, and a proportionate procedure for investigating misuse and restricting accounts. Any enforcement process must comply with applicable law.
